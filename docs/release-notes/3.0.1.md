## 3.0.1 (2026-10-19)

Two `ohlc_1m` columns did not measure what their documentation says. `tick_count_standard` is documented as the number of Standard ticks in the bar, but it always equalled the Raw_Spread count: it was computed as `countIf(... IS NOT NULL)` over an ASOF join that never produces NULLs. `standard_spread_avg` is documented as the average Standard spread in the bar, but it averaged whichever Standard quote preceded each Raw_Spread tick, and the choice among tied quotes could change between rebuilds. Each variant is now aggregated per minute on its own and the two are joined on the minute, so both columns match their definitions, rebuilds are deterministic, and the build no longer runs a per-tick join. On the E2E months the counts now differ on 64,889 of 78,658 bars, where before they were identical on every bar.

### Upgrading

- Bars already stored keep the old values until they are rebuilt. Rebuild an instrument's bars with `update_data(pair, start_date=..., force_redownload=True)`, which re-downloads and regenerates from `start_date`, or call `ExnessDataProcessor().ch_ohlc_generator.regenerate_ohlc(pair)` to rebuild every bar from the stored ticks.

### Fixed

- `tick_count_standard` counts the Standard ticks in the bar's minute (0 when there are none) (#25).
- `standard_spread_avg` is the mean Standard `ask - bid` in the bar's minute (NULL when there are none), deterministic across rebuilds (#25).
- `repo:e2e` checks that `sum(tick_count_raw_spread)` equals the stored Raw_Spread ticks and `sum(tick_count_standard)` equals the Standard ticks in bar minutes, that the two counts differ, and that a sampled `standard_spread_avg` equals a direct per-minute average (#25).
- Stale docstrings describing the v1 30-column schema, normalized metrics and the ASOF join are corrected (#25).

### Changed

- Development runs on Python 3.15.0 and moon 2.6.2; the supported range is unchanged (`requires-python >=3.11`) (#27, #28).
